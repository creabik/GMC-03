## Refund policy

**Our promise**
If we cannot deliver a working licence for the product you bought, you get a full refund. We offer a 30-day refund window from the date of purchase.

**When you get a full refund**
- We cannot deliver your licence details.
- The licence cannot be activated, even after our team has helped you.
- You were charged more than once for the same order.

**When a refund isn't possible**
- The licence has been successfully activated. [Confirm]
- You chose the wrong edition, country, platform or number of users after the licence was delivered. [Confirm]
- The request is made after the 30-day window.

**How to ask for a refund**
Email contact@otickets.shop with your order number and the email address used at checkout, and tell us what went wrong.

**Processing**
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show the refund. [Confirm]

**Contact**
Email contact@otickets.shop or call +1 (907) 622-1000.

## Shipping policy

**Digital delivery, nothing is shipped**
otickets sells digital licences only. Nothing is physically shipped and there are no shipping fees.

**Delivery time**
Your licence details arrive by email within 24 hours of your payment being confirmed.

**What we need from you**
A working email address entered correctly at checkout, the right edition for your business (country, platform and number of users), and a compatible computer (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, for Windows editions; macOS for the Mac edition).

**Where we deliver**
We deliver by email to customers in the United States and Canada.

**If something goes wrong**
Check your spam folder and the email address on your order. If nothing has arrived after 24 hours, email contact@otickets.shop with your order number.

**Contact**
Email contact@otickets.shop or call +1 (907) 622-1000.
